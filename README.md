# SUDARSHAN

A browser-based trainer for recognising, classifying and responding to drone and counter-drone threats, built for unit-level training on ordinary laptops. It is planned around sensor-based scenarios (radar, EO/IR, RF, acoustic), seeded procedural scenario generation, explainable decision-tree scoring, adaptive difficulty and an after-action review dashboard. Prepared for Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement 26247 (Ministry of Defence, Defence Services Staff College).

**Status: planning complete, build not started.** Nothing described here is built yet.

## Honest data

SUDARSHAN is built from open research datasets and published specifications, in fictional scenarios. It uses no real operational or classified data. It makes no claim of validated training effectiveness or doctrine accuracy; any effectiveness result will come only from our own measured pilot.

## Scope and safety

Defensive training only: recognise, classify, respond. The project contains no instructions for building, arming or operating attack drones and no real-world targeting detail. Rules of engagement are a fictional, editable rule set.

## Affiliation

This is an independent student project. It has no official affiliation with the Ministry of Defence, the Defence Services Staff College, DRDO, the Indian armed forces, or any government programme.
