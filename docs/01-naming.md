> **Decision (2026-10-17):** the user kept **SUDARSHAN** after this check. The research below, including the NIGAH recommendation and the association risk, is kept unchanged as the record. See [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) ADR-001.

Method: open web search on 2026-10-17 for official programmes, systems and companies using the name. **Not done:** a formal IP India trade-mark register search (Classes 9, 41, 42). That check is free at the IP India public search (https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/eregister/) and should be run before any public use. Everything below is from public news and reference pages and is cited.

## Verdict

**Switch.** SUDARSHAN is the most loaded possible name for this exact submission. The risk is not legal; it is that an MoD / DSSC evaluator reads it as claiming association with a national air-defence programme.

## Real risk for a submission to MoD

- **Perceived association (high).** "SUDARSHAN: AI counter-drone trainer" on a title slide reads as "the training component of Mission Sudarshan Chakra". Evaluators from the services know the programme. Some will read it as presumptuous, and some as a claim we cannot back. That conflicts with our own honesty rule (locked rule 1).
- **Distinctiveness (high).** Our name is drowned out by a national programme and an operational weapon system, and it is hard to search for or remember as ours.
- **Legal (low to medium, unverified).** A mythological name is not owned by anyone. However, private drone companies already trade under it, and a TM search has not been done. The Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950 protects names listed in its schedule; whether a mission name is covered is **UNVERIFIED**, and we should not need to find out.
- **Does a qualifier fix it?** No. "SUDARSHAN Trainer" makes it worse: it reads as "the trainer for Sudarshan".

Rejected during the search: **ABHYAS** ("practice") is DRDO's high-speed expendable aerial target, itself a drone ([The Defense Post](https://www.thedefensepost.com/2021/12/27/india-abhyas-high-speed-target)). ## Recommendation

1. **Switch to NIGAH.** It names the skill being trained (seeing and judging) rather than a weapon, so it cannot be read as an air-defence programme. It is short and has the best risk profile found. Runner-up: CHAUKAS.
2. Use a descriptor line, not a qualifier inside the name: **NIGAH · Counter-UAS Threat Recognition Trainer**.
3. Before any public use, run the IP India TM search for the chosen name in Classes 9, 41 and 42, and log the result here.
4. Until you decide, the rest of this pack uses "SUDARSHAN (working name)". A rename is a find-and-replace, and the repo will keep the name in one config constant.

## Wordmark direction

- All-caps wordmark in a squared grotesque or engineering sans (for example IBM Plex Sans Condensed SemiBold or Barlow Semi Condensed), tracked +4–6%. One weight only, with no italics.
- A single geometric mark: a thin reticle arc (a 270° circle segment) with one short tick, as on an instrument bezel. It must read at 16 px as a favicon and print in one colour.
- Colour: off-white on graphite (#0E1114 field), with one signal accent (amber #E0A526) used only for the tick. A monochrome version is mandatory.
- Devanagari lock-up in Noto Sans Devanagari at the same cap height, placed beside the Latin, never stacked as decoration.
- Avoid: shields, chakra or wings imagery, the tricolour, the national emblem, service crests, and anything resembling an official insignia (the same association risk again). Also avoid gradients, glows and an AI-sparkle motif.
